Four fixes for citing, all found by using 0.3.0 rather than by reading the code.
Nothing new, and nothing in your vault changes.

## Install

Download **`Sutra_0.3.1_x64-setup.exe`** and run it. Installing over 0.3.0 keeps
everything; no migration runs. The installer is still not code-signed, so
SmartScreen warns on first run: **More info → Run anyway**.

## "Cite a paper" is now "Add paper from Zotero"

It never cited. It opens the picker, which brings a paper **into your vault** —
and then you were left looking at an unchanged paragraph wondering what had
happened, which reads as citing being broken rather than as the button being
misnamed. It is named for what it does now, and both it and the command palette
say where citing actually is.

## To cite a paper in a sentence, type `@`

This was always the answer and was the hardest thing in the app to discover, so
it is now the first thing the manual's Zotero section says. Typing `@` in the
body opens one menu over **both** the sources already in your vault and your
Zotero library. Pick anything in it and the citation lands at the cursor.

If the paper is not in your vault yet, it is brought in on the way — the source
note is created and the citation inserted as a single action, with nothing to
confirm. Papers your vault already has come first and appear instantly, and are
left out of the Zotero results so you cannot import a second copy by accident.
With Zotero closed the menu still works on what your vault already holds.

One rule worth knowing: `@` opens the menu at the start of a line or after a
space, the same as `/`. Typed tight against the previous word it does nothing.

## Picking a paper that fails to import now says so

If Zotero went away between the search and your pressing Enter, the import
failed after the menu had already closed — and the failure was thrown away. All
you saw was a sentence that did not change. It is now reported like every other
failed command, naming the paper it could not bring in.

## Your literature notes are no longer flagged as duplicates of their own sources

Making a literature note deliberately gives it the paper's title, and a source
note carries the same title, so **Possibly the same note** matched them against
each other every time — pointing at the one pair in your vault that is
guaranteed not to be a duplicate. A note and a paper it cites are a reading and
the thing read, and are never offered as duplicates of one another now.

Genuine duplicates are still found. This excludes one pair, not the feature.

## References in IEEE style are readable again

A bibliography rendered as

```
M. A. Farhana et al., &#x201C;Recent advances ...,&#x201D; Journal of Science
```

because the quotation marks arrived from Zotero in a spelling Sutra decoded only
half of. Affected the panel and Word export alike, and any style using those
marks — IEEE most visibly. ACS, RSC and the author-date styles were never
affected, which is why it took a while to notice.

## Still not done

Unchanged from 0.3.0: the installer is unsigned, updates are not automatic,
there is no PDF text extraction and no OCR, numeric citations are numbered per
note rather than across a thesis, and there is no mobile app or sync of its own.
