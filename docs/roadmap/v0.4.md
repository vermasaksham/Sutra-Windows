# v0.4 — evidence from the paper itself

Scope of record. Written so that a later session does not have to reconstruct
what v0.4 was for from a conversation it cannot read.

## The gap v0.4 closes

After v0.3 the bibliographic half of the Zotero integration is mature: two
providers behind one trait, search, item detail, collections, attachment lists,
CSL styling cached per style, re-import that merges rather than overwrites,
offline degradation that says so.

The **evidence** half is empty. `SourcesPanel` gives a page field and a quote
box, and both are filled by copy-typing from the paper. Getting what a source
says out of the source and into a note, with its page attached, is entirely
manual. That is what v0.4 is for.

`eid` — minted in v0.3, one ULID per piece of evidence — exists so that this
could be built without changing the file format again.

## In scope

1. **Zotero annotation import.** Read the highlights and notes a researcher has
   already made in Zotero's reader, with the source they belong to and the page
   they are on, and turn them into evidence entries in a note.
2. **Read-only access to Zotero-managed PDFs**, where the file is on this
   machine. Governed by `docs/decisions/0003-pdf-ownership-and-access.md`:
   ownership is not access, and access transfers nothing.
3. **PDF text extraction with page-aware mapping** — text that knows which page
   it came from, because a quotation without a page is not evidence.
4. **Selection → evidence capture.** Selecting text records the source, the page
   or page range, the exact selected text, and a stable `eid`, without retyping.
5. **Graceful degradation** when a PDF is unavailable. Every failure is named.
   The bibliographic relationship — source note, citation, bibliography — keeps
   working untouched, because it is a ULID in the researcher's markdown and
   depends on no file.
6. **One extraction abstraction** for Zotero-managed PDFs and for PDFs the
   researcher attached into the vault themselves. Same code path where the only
   difference is who owns the file.
7. **Zotero stays the bibliographic authority.** No PDF is copied into the vault
   by default, or as a side effect of reading one.

## Explicitly out of scope

**No AI is required for any of v0.4, and none is used in it.** Annotation
import, path resolution, text extraction, page provenance and evidence capture
are deterministic and work offline whenever the file is locally available. If
they only worked with a model running, they would not be provenance.

Not in v0.4, and not to be started as part of it:

- OCR — a scanned PDF with no text layer degrades to "no text layer", named
- RAG, embeddings, vector or semantic search
- Ask Sutra, or any generated summary of a paper
- AI summarisation of annotations or extracted text
- writing annotations back to Zotero — Zotero remains the only writer of its own
  data
- copying PDFs into the vault (the `source.archive` design in
  `docs/architecture/zotero-pdfs.md` stays unbuilt)

Also not in v0.4, carried over from the chapter-assembly slice: nested chapters,
transclusion, chapter numbering, thesis-wide citation numbering, and a sidebar
rail for chapters.

## Contracts v0.4 must not break

Everything in `docs/architecture/invariants.md` continues to hold. The four
under most pressure from this work, and what holding them means here:

| Invariant                             | What v0.4 must do                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Markdown is the durable knowledge     | Extracted text is derived from a file Sutra does not own. It is never written into the vault. Only evidence the researcher captured is.              |
| SQLite is a rebuildable index         | Extracted text is not "already in a note", so it does not go in the notes index. A separate, disposable cache outside the vault, or no cache at all. |
| Zotero is the bibliographic authority | Sutra does not parse names, invent keys, or reimplement CSL, and now also does not own, alter or copy files.                                         |
| Source ≠ Evidence ≠ Interpretation    | A Zotero annotation's highlighted text is the source's words and becomes a quote. Its comment is the researcher's own words and never becomes one.   |

## Where the work is recorded
